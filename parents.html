LOCKDOJO FOR PARENTS

A two-minute read. Last updated 20 November 2026.

In one line: Lockdojo is a study timer that locks distracting apps (like Instagram, YouTube and games) while your child studies. A cartoon teacher, "Sensei", keeps them company and cheers them on.

The lock is free. There are no ads, no account, and the app has no internet permission, so your child's study data never leaves their phone.

How it works

  1. Your child starts a focus session (for example 25 or 45 minutes).
  2. If they open a distracting app during the session, Lockdojo covers it with a full-screen "Lockdown" wall and sends them back to study.
  3. When the session ends, they see what they did, keep their streak and can see a countdown to their exam.

For a real emergency, holding the wall for 5 seconds opens a 2-minute pass, so your child can always reach you.

Your child's privacy

  • No account, no sign-in. We do not know who your child is.
  • No internet permission. Study sessions, the exam, goals and the name Sensei uses are stored only on the phone.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. No data is sold or shared, because we never receive any.
  • Things only leave the phone if your child chooses to share them (for example a study card to WhatsApp).
  • Settings › Delete all my data wipes everything.

Is the tone safe for children?

Sensei teases in a friendly way ("Caught you. The book is still open!") and always ends kindly. The mentors never insult looks, family, marks or anyone's background, and there is no guilt for a missed day. Reminders are optional and at most once a day.

What Lockdojo is not

Lockdojo is a tool your child chooses to use to study better. It is not spyware or a parental-control app: parents cannot read the child's data, and the child can turn the lock off outside a session. If you need full parental controls, Google Family Link can be used alongside it.

Who makes it

Lockdojo is built by an independent student developer in India who wanted a study app that is strict, fun and honest. Questions? Contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
