LOCKDOJO PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · App id: app.lockdojo · Version 1.9

Short version: Lockdojo keeps your study data on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your study sessions (time, length, how often you left), streak, quests, unlocks and your session cards.
  • Optional things you type: the name Sensei calls you, a "dream line", an exam and its date, session goals, and your "Plan my day" answers (wake, sleep and school times).
  • A Dojo member number. It is a random number made on your phone. It is not an account and no server knows it.
  • Your settings: mentor, theme, music, reminder time, which apps count as study or distraction.

All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your phone.

Android backup

If Android backup is on for your phone, Android can copy one small Lockdojo file (your sessions, settings, name and cards, not your songs) to your own Google account, so it comes back when you move to a new phone. This is Android's own backup; we cannot see it.

App usage while a session runs

If you turn on Usage access, Lockdojo checks which app is in front only while a focus session is running, so the mentor can react and your Log can show where the time went. This stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily study reminder, your phone shows one notification a day at your time. It is made on the phone; nothing is sent.

Ads

Lockdojo has no ads of any kind. The app is paid for only by the optional Dojo Pass and Pro.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you choose to share it. Cards (your Dojo card, session cards, week card), the backup file and the "send my week to my own AI app" text go through Android's share menu to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp, Instagram, Files or Drive). That app's own privacy policy then applies. You can hide your name on shared cards in Settings.

Dojo Pass and Pro

Payments happen inside Google Play. Google handles the payment; we do not see your card or UPI details. The app asks Google Play on your phone which purchases you own.

Children and students

Lockdojo is made for students, including under-18s. Because the app collects no personal data and sends nothing off the phone, it does not build profiles or show ads to anyone. Under 18? Ask a parent before buying anything; Google Play's parental controls also apply.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all my data wipes everything on the phone and the backup file. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
